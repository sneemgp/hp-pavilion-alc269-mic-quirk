# Reading of the numbers, for the report: node 0x12 (Internal Mic) is the
# internal input pin, and its Amp-In caps read ofs=0x00, nsteps=0x03,
# stepsize=0x2f. Step size 0x2f -> (0x2f+1)*0.25 dB = 12 dB per step, and
# nsteps=0x03 means 4 positions: 0/12/24/36 dB -- the full 36 dB range
# alc269_fixup_limit_int_mic_boost() exists to clamp to 0 or 12 dB.
